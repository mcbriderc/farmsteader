# Third-party notices

FarmSteader itself is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0
(see `LICENSE`). It also redistributes the third-party components listed below,
each under its own licence. Those licences are not superseded by the AGPL, and
their terms continue to apply to those files.

The browser libraries are vendored -- committed under `static/vendor/` and
shipped in both the release tarball and the container image -- so this file is
how their copyright notices travel with them. Two of them (Alpine.js and
Turf.js) are minified copies whose original header comments were stripped by the
minifier, which is precisely why this file has to exist: their MIT licences
require the notice to accompany every substantial redistribution.

Python dependencies are *not* listed here. They are installed from PyPI at
deploy time rather than redistributed by this project, so their licences travel
with the packages themselves.

| Component | Version | Licence | Vendored file(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leaflet | 1.9.4 | BSD-2-Clause | `static/vendor/js/leaflet.js`, `static/vendor/css/leaflet.css`, `static/vendor/images/*` |
| Leaflet.draw | 1.0.4 | MIT | `static/vendor/js/leaflet.draw.js`, `static/vendor/css/leaflet.draw.css` |
| htmx | 2.0.4 | 0BSD | `static/vendor/js/htmx.min.js` |
| Alpine.js | 3.14.8 | MIT | `static/vendor/js/alpine.min.js` |
| Turf.js | 7.2.0 | MIT | `static/vendor/js/turf.min.js` |
| Tailwind CSS | 4.3.3 | MIT | compiled into `static/css/dist/styles.css` |

Versions are taken from the files themselves where they record one. Turf.js
does not, so its version was identified by matching the vendored file's SHA-256
against upstream releases -- it is 7.2.0 exactly.

Notes on two entries:

- **htmx** is Zero-Clause BSD, which imposes no attribution requirement at all.
  It is listed for completeness, not obligation.
- **Tailwind CSS** is not vendored as source. The stylesheet is generated by the
  pinned standalone binary, and the generated output embeds Tailwind's preflight
  rules, so its notice is reproduced here.
